# Upvan Terraces, Thane West, Mumbai

A fictional brochure for the LeadOps demo. The project, its RERA number and every figure here are made up.

## Payment plan
20:80 plan: pay ₹2 L on booking, bring the total to 20% within 30 days of the agreement, and pay the remaining 80% at possession. Your bank can release the 80% as one payment when the home is handed over.

## Price and extra charges
The price of ₹1.6 Cr covers this 1,150 sq ft 3 BHK apartment. Stamp duty and registration are extra, at the state government's rates. GST applies at the government's rate for homes under construction. There are no floor-rise or preferred-location charges on this home.

## Specifications
Flooring: Large-format vitrified tiles in the living room, wooden-finish flooring in the main bedroom and anti-skid tiles in the bathrooms.
Kitchen: Quartz platform with a double-bowl sink, full-height dado and provision for a chimney, hob and dishwasher.
Bathrooms: Premium sanitary fittings, a glass shower partition in the main bathroom and a geyser in every bathroom.
Doors and windows: Veneered main door with a digital lock, and UPVC windows with mosquito mesh.
Electrical: Copper wiring, modular switches, air-conditioning points in every room and a power backup of 3 kVA per home.
The structure is an earthquake-resistant RCC frame.

## Parking
This home comes with 2 covered car parking spaces. Visitor parking is at ground level, and every covered space has provision for an electric vehicle charger.

## Maintenance and society
Maintenance is ₹4 per sq ft per month, about ₹4,600 a month for this home, and 18 months are collected in advance at possession. A one-time corpus fund of ₹75 per sq ft goes to the residents' society once it is formed. Maintenance covers security, housekeeping, lifts, common power and the upkeep of the amenities.
