# Indigo Greens, Ravet, Pune

A fictional brochure for the LeadOps demo. The project, its RERA number and every figure here are made up.

## Payment plan
Construction-linked plan: pay ₹2 L on booking, bring the total to 10% at the agreement, then 15% at the plinth, 40% spread evenly across the floor slabs, 15% at brickwork and plaster, 15% at finishes and the last 5% at possession. Each stage is billed only when that work is finished.

## Price and extra charges
The price of ₹89 L covers this 1,240 sq ft 3 BHK apartment. Stamp duty and registration are extra, at the state government's rates. GST applies at the government's rate for homes under construction. There are no floor-rise or preferred-location charges on this home.

## Specifications
Flooring: Vitrified tiles in the living room, bedrooms and kitchen; anti-skid tiles in the bathrooms and balconies.
Kitchen: Granite platform with a stainless steel sink, dado tiles up to 2 feet and provision for a water purifier and exhaust fan.
Bathrooms: Branded sanitary fittings, concealed plumbing and a geyser point in every bathroom.
Doors and windows: Laminated flush doors and powder-coated aluminium sliding windows with mosquito mesh.
Electrical: Copper wiring, modular switches and a power backup of 1 kVA per home for lights and fans.
The structure is an earthquake-resistant RCC frame.

## Parking
This home comes with 2 covered car parking spaces. Visitor parking is at ground level, and every covered space has provision for an electric vehicle charger.

## Maintenance and society
Maintenance is ₹3 per sq ft per month, about ₹3,720 a month for this home, and 24 months are collected in advance at possession. A one-time corpus fund of ₹50 per sq ft goes to the residents' society once it is formed. Maintenance covers security, housekeeping, lifts, common power and the upkeep of the amenities.

## Approvals and home loans
RERA number: DEMO-PUN-003 (fictional). The building plan and commencement approvals are in place. The occupancy certificate is applied for near completion and shared at handover. The project is approved for home loans by leading public and private banks, usually up to 80% of the home's value, and the sales team helps with the paperwork.

## Schools, hospitals, metro and offices nearby
A CBSE school is 1.6 km away and an international school 4.4 km away. A multi-speciality hospital is 4.6 km away. The nearest metro station is 1.9 km away and a supermarket 1.1 km away. The airport is 20.1 km away. Quick access to the Mumbai-Pune Expressway. Construction-linked payment plan. Clubhouse with a co-working lounge.

## Amenities
Residents get: swimming pool, gym, clubhouse, indoor games, rainwater harvesting, co-working lounge. The home is on floor 9 of 22 and faces west.

## Possession and handover
Planned possession is Jun 2027. Construction photos and progress updates are shared every quarter. Defects in the structure are covered for five years after handover, as the RERA rules require.
