# Deccan Skyline, Kokapet, Hyderabad

A fictional brochure for the LeadOps demo. The project, its RERA number and every figure here are made up.

## Payment plan
Construction-linked plan: pay ₹5 L on booking, bring the total to 10% at the agreement, then 15% at the plinth, 40% spread evenly across the floor slabs, 15% at brickwork and plaster, 15% at finishes and the last 5% at possession. Each stage is billed only when that work is finished.

## Price and extra charges
The price of ₹2.6 Cr covers this 2,900 sq ft 4 BHK apartment. Stamp duty and registration are extra, at the state government's rates. GST applies at the government's rate for homes under construction. There are no floor-rise or preferred-location charges on this home.

## Specifications
Flooring: Imported marble in the living and dining rooms, engineered wood in the bedrooms and anti-skid stone on the terraces.
Kitchen: Modular kitchen with quartz counters, built-in hob, chimney and oven, and a separate utility area.
Bathrooms: Designer sanitary fittings, rain showers and a soaking tub in the main bathroom.
Doors and windows: Teak-finish main door with a smart lock, and sound-insulated UPVC windows.
Electrical: Copper wiring, home automation for lights and curtains, central air-conditioning points and full power backup.
The structure is an earthquake-resistant RCC frame.

## Parking
This home comes with 2 covered car parking spaces. Visitor parking is at ground level, and every covered space has provision for an electric vehicle charger.

## Maintenance and society
Maintenance is ₹6 per sq ft per month, about ₹17,400 a month for this home, and 12 months are collected in advance at possession. A one-time corpus fund of ₹150 per sq ft goes to the residents' society once it is formed. Maintenance covers security, housekeeping, lifts, common power and the upkeep of the amenities.
