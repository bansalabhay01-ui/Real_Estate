A fictional brochure for the LeadOps demo. The project, its RERA number and every figure here are made up.

## Payment plan
Construction-linked plan: pay ₹2 L on booking, bring the total to 10% at the agreement, then 15% at the plinth, 40% spread evenly across the floor slabs, 15% at brickwork and plaster, 15% at finishes and the last 5% at possession. Each stage is billed only when that work is finished.

## Price and extra charges
The price of ₹1.28 Cr covers this 1,480 sq ft 3 BHK apartment. Stamp duty and registration are extra, at the state government's rates. GST applies at the government's rate for homes under construction. There are no floor-rise or preferred-location charges on this home.

Kitchen: Quartz platform with a double-bowl sink, full-height dado and provision for a chimney, hob and dishwasher.
Bathrooms: Premium sanitary fittings, a glass shower partition in the main bathroom and a geyser in every bathroom.
Doors and windows: Veneered main door with a digital lock, and UPVC windows with mosquito mesh.
Electrical: Copper wiring, modular switches, air-conditioning points in every room and a power backup of 3 kVA per home.
The structure is an earthquake-resistant RCC frame.

## Parking

A one-time corpus fund of ₹75 per sq ft goes to the residents' society once it is formed. Maintenance covers security, housekeeping, lifts, common power and the upkeep of the amenities.
